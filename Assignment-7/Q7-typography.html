Typography & Text Styling

How type choices shape a readable article

The Art of Clear Communication

Good typography makes information easier to understand. The right combination of font family, size, and spacing gives every paragraph a comfortable rhythm.

A sans-serif font is often used for interfaces because its simple letterforms remain clear at many sizes. Adjusting line height and letter spacing can make longer passages easier to follow.

“Clarity is a design choice.”

Use highlighted phrases sparingly so important ideas stand out. A balanced page combines contrast with enough whitespace to let each section breathe.

Readable type is thoughtful design.
